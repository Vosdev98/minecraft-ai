MinecraftAI

A personal tool that builds scenes in Minecraft, films them, and posts the clips to its owner's own TikTok account.

What it does

  • Picks a structure to build and designs it.
  • Builds it on a private Minecraft server that only the owner runs.
  • Records a short vertical video of the finished build with a scripted camera.
  • Uploads that video to the owner's own TikTok account.

Who it's for

One person: the owner. There are no accounts to sign up for, no website to log into and no other users.
