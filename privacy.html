Privacy Policy

MinecraftAI · last updated 15 October 2026

MinecraftAI runs on one person's computer and serves only that person. It has no users to collect data from, no servers of its own, no accounts and no analytics.

What it stores

  • A TikTok access token for the owner's own account, kept in a file on the owner's computer so the tool can upload videos without signing in every time.
  • The videos it makes, and a short caption for each, also on that computer.
  • A list of what it has built, so it doesn't repeat itself.

What it sends to TikTok

The video file and its caption, plus the token that identifies the owner's account. Nothing else. It reads basic account information (the account's own name and ID) only to confirm it is connected to the right account.

What it does not do

  • No data is collected from anyone other than the owner.
  • Nothing is sold, shared or sent to any third party, advertiser or analytics service.
  • No cookies and no tracking: this website is three static pages.

Keeping and deleting data

Everything stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. Revoking the app's access in TikTok's settings makes the stored token useless at once, and deleting the file removes it.

Children

The tool is not intended for anyone under 13 and collects nothing from them.
